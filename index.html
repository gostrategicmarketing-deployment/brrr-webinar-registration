The method

Five steps. One system.

  1. 1

    Buy

    A house that needs work, with an asset-based lender who cares about the deal, not your paycheck.

  2. 2

    Renovate

    Real numbers from a contractor, line by line.

  3. 3

    Rent

    Rent is 30% more than your mortgage payment. Qualify the tenant.

  4. 4

    Refinance

    On the after repair value, to pay back the first lender.

  5. 5

    Repeat

    The same steps, on the next deal.

Straight from the class

Why most people fail, even when they know the strategy.

Understanding BRRR and executing it are two different things. Thursday's class walks through the five places it goes wrong.

A slide from Cindy's live class.

  1. They don't have the right lenders
  2. They analyze deals wrong
  3. They overestimate the ARVARV: after repair value, what the house is worth once it's fixed
  4. They hire the wrong contractor
  5. They underestimate the rehab

Who it's for

Is Thursday's class for you?

Come to class if

  • You're a beginner who wants to own rentals and has never been shown how investor lenders work.
  • You have some capital or credit to start with, or you're working toward it. The class walks through starting with around $15K.
  • You're open to investing outside your own city, the way Cindy started.
  • You're done collecting information and want the steps in order.

Skip it if

  • You want overnight results. "I do not promise overnight success."
  • You're looking for a flip. This is about owning rentals.
  • You won't do the work. The strategy works if you work the strategy.
